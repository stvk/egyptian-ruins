# egyptian-ruins
A family of intrepid explorers seek to unravel the mysteries of a Japanese wooden box, inside an ancient wooden temple.


This is a HTML5 puzzle written in 2018 for my wife as part of a treasure hunt/puzzle quest to obtain her Christmas present.
Whilst short, it features a couple of clues from our life together that only she would be able to answer, combined with a traditional slide puzzle, and a real world Japanese puzzle box.
